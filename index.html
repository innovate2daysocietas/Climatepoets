How the sound is made

Each month becomes a note. Higher values produce higher pitches within a fixed scale; lower values produce lower pitches. January plays first, then February, and so on. This is a musical representation—not sound recorded by NASA.

05 / SOURCES, METHODS & SPACE SCIENCE

The science behind the sound.

Science is the foundation of ClimatePoets. We distinguish measured observations, musical interpretation, poetic expression and AI-generated art.

EARTH OBSERVATIONS

NASA POWER

The explorer retrieves daily near-surface air temperature (T2M) and corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) from NASA POWER for a selected location and year. Data are summarized into monthly temperature means and precipitation totals.

Visit NASA POWER ↗
DATA SONIFICATION

How numbers become notes

The Jukebox maps the 12 monthly values to pitches across a limited musical scale. The sound is composed from a documented mathematical mapping; it is not an audio recording of space or Earth's atmosphere.

OPEN EARTH VISUALIZATIONS

NASA Earth Information Center

NASA's Earth Information Center provides visualizations explaining changing conditions on our planet. An optional image uploader allows sound exploration of a user's selected visual frame; scientifically accurate sonification requires matching visual values to their underlying data.

Explore Earth Information Center ↗
AI & COMMUNITY

Human creativity, responsible AI

When configured, AI poetry generation receives real loaded observations and separates data-supported statements from metaphor. Human contributors own their work. Submitted poems are moderated before public display; poems, readings and generated artworks should be credited clearly.
