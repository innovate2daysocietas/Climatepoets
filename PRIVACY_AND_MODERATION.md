# ClimatePoets privacy and moderation policy — draft for site owner review

This template is for the site operator to adapt to applicable laws before accepting public submissions. It is not legal advice.

- Anonymous visitors may view published poems and use publicly available NASA data tools. The service may process hosting logs for security and performance.
- Poems saved as drafts remain in the visitor's browser and are not transmitted unless the visitor submits the poem or asks AI services to process the text.
- When a contributor submits a poem, the site receives pen name, poem, theme, optional location, optional email, and the contributor's consent confirmation. Email addresses are for moderation contact and are never included in public gallery responses.
- Moderators review submissions before publication and may reject spam, hate, privacy violations, plagiarism and content that discloses private information.
- If the user requests an AI poem, the relevant monthly NASA measurements and selected theme/tone are sent to the configured AI provider; for art creation, poem text and style are sent to the provider.
- Audio recordings made in the browser are stored locally until the user downloads them. They are not uploaded by this version.
- The site owner must publish their actual contact address, privacy policy, retention/deletion procedures and contributor rights before public launch.
